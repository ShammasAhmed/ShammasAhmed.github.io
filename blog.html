Summer in Los Angeles

This was my first visit to California, and I had so much fun! Exploring LA has been a treat, and I have been pleasently surprised by the public transit. I've had some of the best matcha ever in Koreatown, went to numerous thrifts and fleas in Silverlake, hiked up to Griffith under the sweltering sun, and fallen victim to the local appeal for Pizzeria Sei. I can say for certain that if I were to come to LA again, I wouldn't feel like a stranger!

The Summer Scholars program at DSO, USC Marshall has been an incredibly enlightening experience. I have had the opportunity to learn from academics who are performing cutting-edge research and observe what a research experience looks like at a business school.
